## Schematic vs photo

These are **schematics** — topological lab drawings in the same visual language as our other bring-up boards (Orbitron/Ubuntu, charcoal + teal). They are:

- **Not** photographs
- **Not** CAD / Gerber exports
- **Not** copies or traces of vendor documentation
- **Not** measurement displays (no temperatures, hashrates, or other figures)

Seat counts and placements are illustrative of the bring-up topology we discuss in the worklog and matrix (control board + power + cooling + chain-attached hashboards). They are not a claim about a specific SKU’s population or silkscreen.
