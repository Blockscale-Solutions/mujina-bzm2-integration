RDS 2.0 · control-board schematic

Topology for bring-up.
Schematic, not a photo.

Original stylized drawing of the RDS 2.0 control plane we are porting Mujina onto: power supply, fans, the control board, and hashboard chain seats. Geometry is topological. It is not a CAD export, not a vendor figure, and not a measurement display.

  • Power
  • Fan PWM / sense
  • Hashboard chain UART
  • Control board

What this shows

  • PSU — system power into the control board.
  • Fans — PWM / sense headers driven from the control board.
  • RDS 2.0 control board — host/MCU, fan headers, power in, chain UART ports.
  • HB0–HB2 — hashboard chain seats (BZM2 ASIC boards). Seat count is illustrative of a multi-board chain, not a claim about a specific SKU population.

What this is not

  • Not a photograph or a traced PCB.
  • Not a paste from vendor documentation.
  • Not a live telemetry panel — heat/status affordances exist for later wiring; this page publishes no temperatures or hashrates.
